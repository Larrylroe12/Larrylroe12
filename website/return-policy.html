Return Policy

Shop with confidence. If something isn't right, we'll make it right.

30-Day Return Window

We accept returns within 30 days of the delivery date. To be eligible for a return, items must be:

  • Unworn and in new condition
  • In the original packaging (box, tags, tissue paper)
  • Free from signs of wear, odors, or damage

Items that do not meet these conditions will not be accepted for return.

How to Start a Return

To initiate a return, follow these steps:

  • Email us at support@ballerscloset911.com with your order number and reason for return.
  • We will respond within 1–2 business days with return instructions and a return authorization number.
  • Securely package the item(s) with the original packaging and include your order number inside.
  • Ship the item(s) to the address provided in our return instructions.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your return, we will notify you by email. If approved, your refund will be processed to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

Original shipping costs are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (e.g., wrong item sent, defective product).

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? We're happy to help with an exchange, subject to availability. Contact us within 30 days of delivery to request an exchange. We'll ship the replacement once we receive your original item.

Defective or Incorrect Items

If you received a defective item or the wrong product, please contact us within 7 days of delivery. We will cover the cost of return shipping and send you the correct item at no additional charge.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned:

  • Items marked as "Final Sale"
  • Items that have been worn, washed, or altered
  • Items without original packaging or tags
  • Gift cards

Questions?

If you have any questions about our return policy, please contact us. We're here to help.

Still Have Questions?

Our support team is happy to help with any return or exchange request.
